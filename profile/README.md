### Building practical technology for ambitious businesses

**OneGate Qatar** is a Qatar-based technology company developing digital platforms and software solutions. Our work spans restaurant technology, mapping tools, commerce experiences, and the infrastructure that supports them.

We focus on **maintainable architecture, thoughtful product design, security-conscious engineering, and reliable delivery**—with solutions shaped for real operational needs.

### CoreGate · Engineering beyond the interface

**[CoreGate](https://coregate.tech/)** is our engineering-focused web presence, presenting practical capabilities in **software architecture, AI systems and agents, workflow automation, complex integrations, cloud infrastructure, and technical consulting**.

> Build it right. Make it scale. Keep it running.

Our approach emphasizes understanding requirements and failure modes, choosing architecture based on real trade-offs, verifying implementations, and operating dependable systems.

### How we approach engineering

| Principle | What it means to us |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Design for maintainability** | Clear boundaries, documented decisions, and manageable complexity. |
| **Build with security in mind** | Least privilege, sensible controls, and careful handling of sensitive information. |
| **Make quality visible** | Reviews, automated checks, and evidence-based release decisions. |
| **Improve deliberately** | Measure problems, fix root causes, and keep systems operable. |
